An Architect at the Intersection

At A Thousand Suns Academy, the mandate was encoding esoteric and ancient wisdom into secure, scalable modern infrastructure — a Flutter mobile app governed by a 10-service AWS stack, identity secured through OAuth 2.0, SAML, and JWT, and a media pipeline that moves sacred content from recording to platform without losing integrity.

At Billion Readers, the work extended to indigenous cultural preservation. The FirstSongs initiative uses AI tools like Suno to bring indigenous songs to professional audio quality and embed them in archival infrastructure designed for longevity. AI skill-share sessions drove organisational adoption of Claude, Adobe Firefly, and Gemini — transforming how a cross-country nonprofit collaborates and contracts at national scale.

At Invisible Technologies, the standard was different in kind: data quality assurance for text-to-vision model training pipelines supporting tier-one frontier artificial intelligence laboratories, where correctness is not optional. What a model learns is determined entirely by what it is shown.

The Thread

A living paradox.

A security architect who writes the music that plays on the internet. A keeper of ancient and esoteric wisdom who governs the cloud infrastructure that carries it. A specialist in the quality of what machines are taught to see, who also builds the tools that preserve what human eyes once feared would be lost forever.

The rarest things cannot coexist — except they do, here, in one body of work. Speed and quality. Precision and feeling. The frontier of what is possible and the depth of what has always been true. A financial institution and a fractal. An indigenous song and a language model. A change governance framework and a song about the void.

"The thread is not contradiction. It is the accumulation of multiple theories of everything, held by one standard: integrity — in the data, in the systems, in the people on either side of every decision."

Precision + Poetry

The same mind that validates what a machine learns to see also writes the music that plays when you close your eyes. These are not opposites. They are the same impulse.

Ancient + Emergent

Cloud infrastructure in service of esoteric wisdom. AI tools in service of indigenous songs. The newest thing preserving the oldest. The boundary between them is where the work lives.

Scale + Soul

Systems that reach national television and tier-one frontier artificial intelligence laboratories. Tools that give a single team member three weeks of their life back. Both matter. Neither is more important than the other.
